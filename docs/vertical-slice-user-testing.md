# Vertical Slice — User Testing Kit (Harmony Harbor)

> **The one question:** *Will a 5–7-year-old want to keep playing?*
> Build: the playable web slice at the repo root (`index.html` → `harbor.js`). Open on a phone/tablet, sound ON.
> Loop tested: **Explore → Meet Rumi → Learning challenge (no-fail early reading) → Befriend Twinkle → Harmony Energy → Rumi's transformation → Reward → "Again?"**

---

## 1. How to run it
- **Device:** the child's normal tablet/phone, **volume up** (narration is core). Headphones optional.
- **Length:** one playthrough ≈ **5–8 minutes**.
- **Setup:** open the link, hand the device to the child at the "Tap to Start" screen. Adult sits beside, **does not coach unless the child is stuck >20s**.
- **Sessions:** test **5–8 children** individually (5, 6, and 7-year-olds). One playthrough each; offer "Again?" and note if they take it.

---

## 2. Test script for parents (read aloud, warm & neutral)

> "Hi! I have a new game about a singing hero named Rumi. I'd love your help trying it — there are **no wrong answers**, and you can't lose. I'm going to watch, but you're the player. Ready? Tap the big star to start!"

During play, **only** say:
- If stuck >20s: *"What do you think you could tap?"* (never give the answer).
- If they look unsure: *"You're doing great — take your time."*
- Nothing else. Let the game's narration teach.

After play:
> "That was awesome watching you! Can I ask you a few quick questions?" → use §5.

**Parent's golden rule:** resist helping. We're testing whether *the game* (not the parent) carries a 5-year-old through. Let them struggle a little; note where.

---

## 3. Success metrics (go / iterate / no-go)

| Metric | How measured | 🎯 Target (GO) | Iterate | No-go |
|--------|--------------|----------------|---------|-------|
| **Completion** | finished loop unaided | ≥ 80% finish solo | 60–79% | < 60% |
| **"Play again?"** (the headline) | taps *Another Adventure!* | ≥ 70% choose again | 50–69% | < 50% |
| **Unprompted delight** | smiles/laughs/gasps/"whoa!" | ≥ 3 moments/child | 1–2 | 0 |
| **Reading engagement** | attempts each round, uses 🔊 | actively tries all 3 rounds | skips/taps randomly | disengages |
| **Transformation reaction** | observed excitement at Rumi's glow-up | "wow"/points/re-watch wish ≥ 80% | mild | none |
| **Twinkle attachment** | reaction to befriending | names/hugs-screen/"so cute" ≥ 70% | neutral | ignores |
| **Time-to-first-smile** | stopwatch from Start | < 30s | 30–60s | > 60s |
| **Frustration events** | confusion/visible upset | ≤ 1 brief, self-recovered | 2–3 | rage/quit |
| **Independence** | adult interventions needed | ≤ 1 | 2–3 | constant |

**Emotional-arc check (the 5 goals):** tally at least one observed beat of each — **Curiosity** (explore/tap around), **Discovery** (finds Gloomling/sparkles), **Success** (completes a round), **Pride** (reaction to reward/star), **Excitement** (transformation). Target: **all 5 present** for ≥ 70% of kids.

---

## 4. Observation checklist (tick during play)

**Onboarding**
- ☐ Understood "Tap to Start" without help
- ☐ Listened to Rumi's intro (didn't skip blindly)
- ☐ Knew to walk (tapped the ground) within 15s

**Exploration**
- ☐ Moved the character purposefully
- ☐ Noticed/collected sparkles (delight)
- ☐ Followed the gold ring marker to the Gloomling

**Learning challenge**
- ☐ Understood each round from **audio alone** (pre-reader test)
- ☐ Used 🔊 "Hear it" and/or 💡 "Hint" when unsure
- ☐ Round 1 (letter sound) — solo? hint? modeled?
- ☐ Round 2 (word↔picture) — solo? hint? modeled?
- ☐ Round 3 (blend d-o-g) — understood "tap in order"?
- ☐ Reacted positively to correct answers (the praise/chime landed)
- ☐ **Never appeared to "fail"** / never upset by a wrong tap
- ☐ Adaptive easing triggered? (did they get the 2-option / modeled help?)

**Reward loop**
- ☐ Reacted to the harbor blooming + lighthouse relighting
- ☐ Tapped/loved Twinkle
- ☐ **Reacted to Rumi's transformation** (the payoff)
- ☐ Understood the reward cards (Star / Twinkle / Harmony)

**Close**
- ☐ Chose "Another Adventure!" (or asked to replay)
- ☐ Wanted to show someone / talk about it

**Friction log (free text):** _where did they pause, mis-tap, or look to the adult?_

---

## 5. Key questions to ask children (after play)

Keep it playful, use pointing/picture answers, don't lead. Smiley scale 😢🙂😄 where rating.

1. **"Did you have fun?"** (😢/🙂/😄) — the headline feeling.
2. **"Do you want to play again?"** (yes / maybe / no) — *the* question.
3. **"What was your favorite part?"** (open — listen for: Twinkle / transformation / reading / walking / sparkles).
4. **"Who is Rumi? What is she good at?"** — does the fantasy + her reading specialty land?
5. **"What did you help do?"** — did they grasp *they* relit the lighthouse by reading?
6. **"Tell me about your new friend."** (Twinkle) — attachment + memorability.
7. **"What happened to Rumi at the end?"** — was the transformation memorable/exciting?
8. **"Was anything tricky or confusing?"** — surface friction.
9. **"Would you want a Twinkle stuffie?"** (yes/no) — toyetic/franchise signal.
10. **Next day (if possible):** *"Remember the harbor game? What do you remember?"* — 24h recall of Rumi/Twinkle/reading.

---

## 6. What this slice deliberately does NOT test
Hub world, multiple districts, other heroes, dress-up, pet care, progression depth, accounts, monetization, parent dashboard. (Per scope — *do not build future systems yet.*) If kids love this loop, **then** we expand.

## 7. Decision rule
- **GO (build more):** ≥70% "play again," all 5 emotional beats common, completion ≥80%, transformation excitement high.
- **ITERATE:** loop works but a beat underperforms (e.g., reading round too hard, transformation underwhelming) → tune that beat, retest.
- **RETHINK:** kids don't re-engage or can't follow audio → revisit core fantasy/onboarding before investing further.

> Capture short **video** (with consent) of faces during the transformation and the "Again?" moment — those two clips tell you almost everything.

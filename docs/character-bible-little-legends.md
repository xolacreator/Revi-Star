# Character Bible — *Starbound Hunters: Little Legends* (Ages 5–7 Edition)

> **Audience:** children ages 5–7 · **Tone:** bright, kind, empowering, magical · **No violence.**
> **Built from the HUNTRIX concept sheet** (Rumi, Mira, Zoey), adapted to be age-appropriate, then expanded to **6 heroes** so each one models a different **learning strength**.
>
> **The gentle reframe:** the HUNTRIX trio are **Star Hunters** — a K-pop group whose music has real magic. They don't fight monsters; they **sing the sadness out of "Gloomlings"** and turn them into happy little friends called **Star Pals**. Their "weapons" are **light tools** that draw glowing shapes, light dark paths, and make music — never harm anyone.

**Six strengths, six heroes:** Reading · Logic · Rhythm · Math · Creativity · Memory.
The message to every child: *your favorite way of being smart is a superpower — and there's a hero who shines just like you.*

# RUMI — "The Voice" 🎤
**Learning Strength: Reading & Words**

**Personality.** Warm, encouraging, the big-sister leader. Rumi believes everyone has a song inside them. She's brave but gentle, and she always makes room for quieter friends to be heard.

**Strengths.** Reading signs and storybooks, sounding out new words, leading the group, calming nervous friends, beautiful singing.

**Learning specialty.** *Reading & early literacy.* Rumi helps kids recognize letters, sound out words, and read the magical signs around the city. Her missions hide reading inside adventure ("help the lost Gloomling read the map home!").

**Favorite activities.** Story time, writing songs, naming new Star Pals, decorating the group's clubhouse with word-banners.

**Signature music style.** Uplifting **anthem-pop** — big sing-along choruses with clear, simple lyrics kids can read on screen and shout along to.

**Emotional growth arc.** Rumi starts out thinking a good leader has to do everything herself. Across the story she learns that **listening** is just as powerful as singing — that leading means helping *others* find their voice.

**Transformation stages.**
1. **Trainee Rumi** — everyday jacket, humming quietly to herself.
2. **Star Hunter Rumi** — her braid and jacket glow gold; the Songstaff lights up with floating letters.
3. **Starbound Voice** — a radiant stage forms beneath her; her words appear in the sky as glowing letters everyone can read.

**Catchphrases.**
- "Let's read our way there!"
- "Every word is a little spark."
- "I hear you — and you matter."

**Friendship dynamics.** Rumi + **Zoey** are best-friend opposites (calm leader + bouncy spark) — their **duet** is the team's biggest sing-along. Rumi gently mentors **Remi**, helping her speak up.

**Mentor relationships.** Mentored by **Director Luna** (see Mentors). Rumi in turn is the "first mentor" every new player meets — she narrates the friendly tutorial.

# MIRA — "The Blade" ⚔️→✨
**Learning Strength: Logic & Problem-Solving**

**Personality.** Cool, confident, a little bit of playful attitude — the brave one who runs *toward* a tricky puzzle. Loyal and protective of her friends, especially the shy ones.

**Strengths.** Figuring out puzzles, finding patterns, planning a path, staying calm when things look hard, lighting the way for others.

**Learning specialty.** *Logic & problem-solving.* Mira's **Starlight Blade** draws glowing lines that connect clues, sort shapes, and solve "which-one-doesn't-belong" and maze puzzles. (Her blade is a **light tool** — it lights paths and draws shapes, it never hurts anyone.)

**Favorite activities.** Mazes, matching games, building light-bridges, "spot the difference," leading the team through tricky districts.

**Signature music style.** Bold, cool **hip-hop / dance-pop** with a steady, confident beat — the "you've got this" track.

**Emotional growth arc.** Mira begins impatient — she wants to solve everything *fast* and sometimes leaves friends behind. She learns that **the best solutions are found together**, and that going slow enough to include everyone makes her even braver.

**Transformation stages.**
1. **Trainee Mira** — graphic tee, arms crossed, ready for anything.
2. **Star Hunter Mira** — crimson hair streams with light; the Starlight Blade traces glowing puzzle-lines in the air.
3. **Starbound Strategist** — a grid of light unfolds around her; she can light up a whole puzzle-path at once for her friends to follow.

**Catchphrases.**
- "Tricky? I love tricky!"
- "Let's figure it out — together."
- "Follow the light!"

**Friendship dynamics.** Mira + **Nova** are the "thinking team" (logic + numbers) — their duet solves the biggest puzzles. Mira looks out for **Indie**, reminding her that even big dreams need a first step.

**Mentor relationships.** Mentored by **Director Luna**. Mira mentors brand-new players in the puzzle districts, cheering: "Mistakes are just clues!"

# ZOEY — "The Beat" 🥁
**Learning Strength: Rhythm & Music**

**Personality.** Bouncy, joyful, the youngest-feeling spark of the group. Zoey turns *everything* into a beat — walking, cleaning up, even waiting in line. Pure fun energy with a big heart.

**Strengths.** Keeping a beat, dancing, copying and creating patterns, cheering everyone up, making any task feel like a game.

**Learning specialty.** *Rhythm & music — and patterns.* Zoey's **Beat Stars** bounce in tap-tap-clap patterns kids repeat back (tap / hold / clap), building rhythm, sequencing, and listening skills — the foundation of early reading and math, too.

**Favorite activities.** Dance-alongs, clap-back games, drumming on anything, making up silly songs, the Performance Stage.

**Signature music style.** Energetic **dance & percussion pop** — claps, stomps, and call-and-response beats made for small hands to tap along.

**Emotional growth arc.** Zoey starts out so excited she rushes ahead and loses the beat. She learns that **focus and listening** make the music even better — that the magic is in the *pause* as much as the *tap*.

**Transformation stages.**
1. **Trainee Zoey** — sneakers tapping, can't sit still.
2. **Star Hunter Zoey** — purple streaks glow; Beat Stars orbit her in a rhythm ring.
3. **Starbound Beat** — the whole stage lights up in time with her; she leads a city-wide dance that wakes the Frequency.

**Catchphrases.**
- "Tap-tap, you got it!"
- "If you can clap it, you can do it!"
- "Feel the beat — that's the magic!"

**Friendship dynamics.** Zoey + **Rumi** are the heart of the group's big numbers. Zoey pulls calm **Remi** onto the dance floor; Remi keeps Zoey from losing the count. Perfect balance.

**Mentor relationships.** Mentored by **DJ Echo** (a fun rhythm mentor — see Mentors). Zoey hosts every Performance Stage tutorial.

# NOVA — "The Spark" ✨🔢
**Learning Strength: Math & Counting**

**Personality.** Curious, cheerful, always counting things ("three clouds, four stars!"). Nova is delighted by numbers and loves to share that delight. A little perfectionist at first — learns to laugh at mistakes.

**Strengths.** Counting, comparing (more/less, big/small), simple adding, sorting star gems, noticing how many.

**Learning specialty.** *Math & counting.* Nova's **Star Counter** gathers glowing star gems; missions count, group, and compare them ("collect 5 red gems, then 2 more — how many now?"). Number magic, hidden in adventure.

**Favorite activities.** Collecting star gems, counting Star Pals at bedtime, sorting things by color and size, treasure hunts.

**Signature music style.** Bubbly **bubblegum-pop** with counting hooks ("one-two-three, sparkle with me!").

**Emotional growth arc.** Nova thinks every answer must be perfect and gets frustrated by mistakes. She learns the team's golden rule: **"A wrong try is just a step closer."** Counting again isn't failing — it's how you get there.

**Transformation stages.**
1. **Trainee Nova** — counting on her fingers, ponytail bouncing.
2. **Star Hunter Nova** — golden numbers swirl around her; the Star Counter glows with gathered gems.
3. **Starbound Spark** — a galaxy of countable stars blooms overhead; she can light constellations by number.

**Catchphrases.**
- "Let's count it out!"
- "One more try, one step closer."
- "Numbers are just stars in a row!"

**Friendship dynamics.** Nova + **Mira** = the thinking team. Nova helps **Indie** count out her colors; Indie helps Nova see that there's more than one right answer.

**Mentor relationships.** Mentored by **Director Luna**. Nova guides the star-gem counting districts.

# INDIE — "The Dreamer" 🎨
**Learning Strength: Creativity & Art**

**Personality.** Imaginative, free-spirited, sees a rainbow in everything. Indie has a hundred ideas a minute and a paint smudge on her cheek. Big dreamer with the biggest heart.

**Strengths.** Designing outfits, mixing colors, inventing, pretend-play, making something beautiful out of anything, comforting friends with a handmade gift.

**Learning specialty.** *Creativity, color & shape.* Indie runs the **Style Studio** (dress-up): designing costumes teaches colors, shapes, patterns, and self-expression. Her **Color Brush** paints light back into gray Gloom-zones.

**Favorite activities.** Designing Concepts (outfits), painting, decorating the clubhouse, dreaming up new Star Pal accessories.

**Signature music style.** Dreamy, sparkly **synth-pop** — soft and whimsical, like daydreaming out loud.

**Emotional growth arc.** Indie starts a hundred projects and finishes none — she flits from idea to idea. She learns the joy of **finishing one thing**, and that a dream you complete can light up someone's whole day.

**Transformation stages.**
1. **Trainee Indie** — paint-splashed overalls, sketchbook in hand.
2. **Star Hunter Indie** — ribbons of rainbow light trail her brush; gray streets bloom with color where she dances.
3. **Starbound Dreamer** — she paints an entire sky-mural of light; her finished masterpiece becomes a stage for the whole team.

**Catchphrases.**
- "Let's make it beautiful!"
- "There's no wrong color in a rainbow."
- "Finish the dream — then dream a new one!"

**Friendship dynamics.** Indie + **Remi** balance each other: Indie brings wild ideas, Remi remembers and organizes them into something real. Mira keeps Indie grounded with "first step first."

**Mentor relationships.** Mentored by **Auntie Iris**, the kind costume-maker (see Mentors). Indie hosts the dress-up/Style Studio.

---

# REMI — "The Keeper" 🦉
**Learning Strength: Memory**

**Personality.** Calm, thoughtful, observant — the one who remembers everyone's birthday and where the lost toy is. A little shy at first, with a quiet, steady confidence that grows.

**Strengths.** Remembering sequences, songs, and faces; noticing details; staying patient; being the reliable friend.

**Learning specialty.** *Memory & sequencing.* Remi's **Memory Locket** plays back patterns to repeat (light-up "do-it-back" games), match-the-pairs, and "what changed?" — strengthening working memory through performance routines.

**Favorite activities.** Memory match games, learning dance routines step-by-step, keeping the team's scrapbook, quiet puzzles.

**Signature music style.** Smooth, looping **R&B / chill-pop** — gentle repeating melodies that are easy to remember and hum back.

**Emotional growth arc.** Remi remembers everything but is too shy to share what she knows. She learns to **speak up** — that her good memory helps the whole team when she's brave enough to say "I remember the way!"

**Transformation stages.**
1. **Trainee Remi** — tidy, watchful, hugging her locket.
2. **Star Hunter Remi** — glowing memory-lights circle her like fireflies, replaying the team's best moments.
3. **Starbound Keeper** — she replays a whole song of light from memory, guiding everyone home through the dark.

**Catchphrases.**
- "I remember — follow me!"
- "Watch closely… now your turn!"
- "Little by little, I've got it."

**Friendship dynamics.** Remi + **Zoey** (memory + rhythm) make the best routines. Rumi helped Remi find her voice; Remi now helps shy new players feel brave.

**Mentor relationships.** Mentored by **DJ Echo**. Remi guides the memory-routine districts.

## Mentors (the grown-up guides)

| Mentor | Role | Warmth they model |
|--------|------|-------------------|
| **Director Luna** | The kind leader of the Star Hunters' agency. Calm, wise, believes in every child. | *Encouragement, patience, "try again."* |
| **DJ Echo** | The playful music mentor who runs the Performance Stage. | *Joy, focus, rhythm, having fun while learning.* |
| **Auntie Iris** | The cozy costume-maker who runs the Style Studio. | *Creativity, self-expression, kindness.* |

**Mentorship style (important for ages 5–7):** mentors never scold and never "win" for the child. They **ask gentle questions** ("What do you notice?"), **celebrate effort over results**, and **hand the spotlight to the child**. Older heroes mentor younger-feeling ones (Rumi→Remi, Mira→Indie), modeling that *everyone is both a learner and a helper.*

## Friendship web (how they fit together)

```
        RUMI (Reading) ──── duet ──── ZOEY (Rhythm)
          │  mentors                    │  balances
        REMI (Memory) ──── routines ── /
          │  balances
        INDIE (Creativity) ── ideas+order ── REMI
          │  grounded by
        MIRA (Logic) ──── thinking team ──── NOVA (Math)
```

- **No rivalries, no mean kids.** Every relationship is supportive. Conflicts are small and kind ("Zoey rushed ahead — let's help her slow down"), always resolved with empathy.
- **Duets** = two heroes performing together, which the game uses for co-op moments and to show *different strengths combining* (the core social-emotional lesson).

## How the learning is "hidden in the adventure" (for designers & parents)

| Hero | Skill | Sample mission verb |
|------|-------|---------------------|
| Rumi | Reading | "Help the Gloomling read the sign to find home." |
| Mira | Logic | "Light the path through the maze — which way is right?" |
| Zoey | Rhythm | "Tap the beat to wake the sleepy stage." |
| Nova | Math | "Gather 5 star gems, then 3 more — how many shine?" |
| Indie | Creativity | "Design the perfect outfit to cheer up a friend." |
| Remi | Memory | "Watch the light-song, then play it back." |

**Every mission ends the same way:** the Gloomling isn't beaten — it's **helped**, and it transforms into a happy **Star Pal** the child collects. The lesson under every level: *being kind and using your strengths makes the world brighter.*

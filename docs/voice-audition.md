# 🎧 Voice Audition Kit — pick a voice for each character

We're choosing the **actual voice** for each character first. You'll paste a sample line
into ElevenLabs, try a few voices, and pick your favorite. Send me your picks and I'll
generate every line in those voices and wire them into the game.

> Goal: each character sounds **distinct, kid-friendly, and human** — warm, not robotic.

---

## How to audition (5 minutes, no tech needed)
1. Go to **elevenlabs.io** → sign up (free tier is enough to audition).
2. Open **"Text to Speech"** (the main studio) — or browse **"Voices → Voice Library."**
3. **Paste the character's sample line** (below) into the text box.
4. Click different voices to hear the line. When one feels right, note its **name**.
5. **Kid-friendly tuning** (sliders on the right): set **Stability ≈ 45–55**, **Style ≈ 0–15**,
   **Similarity ≈ 80**. Lower Style = more natural/less theatrical. That's the sweet spot for
   warm, gentle, child-appropriate delivery.
6. Write your pick on the line under each character. (Voice **name** is enough — or the
   voice **ID** if you see it.)

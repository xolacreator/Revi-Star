# Character Evolution System — Learning Powers Transformation

> *Children should be excited to learn because it helps their favorite hero grow.*
> **5 evolution stages per hero.** Each stage unlocks **new costumes, animations, dance moves, visual effects, songs, and story content.**
> Supersedes the 3-stage sketch in the Character Bible (those map to stages 1/3/5).

---

## 1. The core idea

Every hero (and the player's own avatar) climbs a **Stardom Path** of **5 transformations**, fueled entirely by **learning in that hero's domain**. Mastering reading grows Rumi; mastering math grows Nova; and the player's avatar grows from *all* learning. The transformation is a big, sparkly, earned magical-girl-style glow-up — the emotional payoff that makes a child *want* the next lesson.

**Promise to the child:** "When I learn, my hero becomes more amazing — and so do I."

## 2. The 5-stage ladder (shared structure, themed per hero)

| Stage | Name | Unlocked by (domain mastery) | Feeling |
|-------|------|------------------------------|---------|
| **1** | **Trainee** | starting out | belonging |
| **2** | **Rising Star** | first skills mastered (~Discover band cleared) | momentum |
| **3** | **Star Hunter** | core skills mastered (~Practice band) | confidence |
| **4** | **Starlight Idol** | advanced skills (~Master band begun) | pride |
| **5** | **Starbound Legend** | domain mastery + helped the whole district | radiance |

**Each evolution unlocks all six:** 👗 costume · 🎞️ idle/emote animations · 💃 a new dance move · ✨ signature VFX · 🎵 a new song/verse · 📖 a story beat.

### Evolution mechanic (data-driven, no-grind, no-pay)
- A hero's **Stardom meter** fills with **Star Gems** earned from learning missions in their domain.
- Reaching a threshold triggers a **Transformation cutscene** (one-time, rewatchable) + permanent unlocks.
- **100% earned by learning. Not purchasable. No combat. No power stats** — transformations are *cosmetic + content + emotional*, never "stronger in battle" (there is no battle).
- Re-watch any transformation anytime in the **Stardom Gallery** (collection pride).

## 4. UX & content notes
- **Transformation cutscenes:** 6–10s, skippable, rewatchable; the marquee reward — *this is the moment kids screenshot/share* (parent-gated export).
- **Gallery:** all stages, outfits, dances, songs viewable in Friendship Plaza (collection + replay value).
- **Never a step backward:** evolutions only add; no decay, no "level loss."
- **Pacing:** stage-ups land roughly weekly for an engaged child (tuned with the mastery model in `learning-design.md`), so there's always a "next glow-up" on the horizon — without grinding or paying.
- **Data:** `schemas/evolution_stage.schema.json` → `{heroId, stage, unlock:{costume,anim,dance,vfx,song,story}, requirement:{skillDomain, masteryThreshold, districtHarmony}}`.

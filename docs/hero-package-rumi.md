> **Selected hero:** Rumi · Learning specialty: **Reading & Words** · Home district: **Harmony Harbor**
> Visual foundation: the HUNTRIX concept sheet + [`art-bible.md`](art-bible.md). Audience: **ages 5–7**.

## PHASE 1 — Character Foundation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Rumi (RU-mee) · stage title **"The Voice"** |
| **Age range read** | Reads ~8–9 ("big-kid" the audience looks *up* to, not a peer or an adult) |
| **Personality** | Warm, encouraging big-sister leader. Brave but gentle; believes everyone has a song inside them. Makes room for quieter friends. |
| **Learning specialty** | **Reading & early literacy** — letters, sounds, sight words, reading signs/stories, singing lyrics. |
| **Favorite activity** | Story time and writing songs; naming new Star Pals; reading aloud to make the shy ones smile. |
| **Strengths** | Reading & sounding out words, leading, calming nerves, beautiful singing, lifting others up. |
| **Growth challenges** | Thinks a good leader must do everything herself; learning to **listen** and to let others find *their* voice. |
| **Signature colors** | **Lighthouse Gold `#FFC83D`** + **Royal Purple `#7B4FC4`** + clean **White `#FFFFFF`**; gold = her glow. |
| **Signature symbol** | **The Lyric Star** — a rounded 5-point gold star with a tiny soundwave/ribbon curl trailing off one point (appears on her mic, as her UI icon, in VFX, and on merch). |
| **Emotional arc** | Reluctant face of the label → discovers that *leading means helping everyone be heard* → becomes a radiant voice for the whole city. |

**Why Rumi resonates with ages 5–7.** She's the **aspirational older kid** every preschooler wants to be near — kind, capable, and *sees you*. Her power is literally **reading and singing**, the exact skills a 5–7-year-old is proud to be mastering, so her growth mirrors theirs ("I can read that sign — like Rumi!"). Her warmth removes fear of failure; her gold glow and Lyric Star are instantly recognizable and toyetic; and her "everyone has a song" message models confidence and inclusion. She is the safe, smiling front door to the whole franchise.

## PHASE 2 — Visual Design Package

*Art-directable description for a professional illustrator. Style: stylized 3D toon, Pixar/Disney-Jr warmth, softened from the teen sheet for ages 5–7 (rounder, ~4.7 heads).*

### HEAD
- **Face shape:** soft rounded heart — full cheeks, gently tapered little chin (no sharp jaw).
- **Eye shape:** large, rounded almond, slight upward outer tilt (friendly, confident).
- **Eye size:** big — ~⅓ of face height; bright, with a large catch-light + a small secondary sparkle (her "shine").
- **Iris color:** warm amber-gold to tie to her palette.
- **Eyelashes:** soft, clumped upper lashes (3 stylized lash tips at the outer corner); no heavy/mature lashes.
- **Eyebrows:** medium-thick, smooth, expressive; purple-tinted to match hair; high mobility for emotion.
- **Nose:** tiny soft button — a single shape/highlight, barely there.
- **Mouth shape:** wide and mobile, made for singing; full but simple lips, kid-soft.
- **Smile design:** signature **open warm smile** — confident, inviting, shows top teeth in a soft row; cheeks lift to crowd the lower eyelids ("Duchenne" warmth).

### HAIR
- **Overall silhouette:** her franchise read — **one long, thick braid** swept over one shoulder, with a soft swooped fringe. Unmistakable in pure black.
- **Front view:** rounded fringe parted slightly off-center, two face-framing strands; braid draping forward over the left shoulder.
- **Side view:** braid hangs to mid-thigh with chunky, readable plait segments; gentle volume at the crown.
- **Back view:** braid runs center-back in 5–6 large plait beads; a small gold star tie at the top and tip.
- **Color treatment:** rich **royal purple** base with a slightly lighter violet midtone; **gold ribbon** woven through the braid.
- **Highlight treatment:** one broad soft highlight band across the crown + braid (toon 2-band shading, not noisy strands); gold-glow rim when transformed.
- **Readability from distance:** purple braid + gold = a single bold two-color shape; works at icon size.

### BODY
- **Head-to-body ratio:** **~4.7 heads** (big-kid hero, not toddler, not teen).
- **Height:** tallest of the trio (leader read), but still soft and youthful.
- **Build:** slim-but-soft, no muscle/curve emphasis; comfortable, active kid silhouette.
- **Hands:** slightly oversized, simple **4-finger** mitten-friendly hands (clear, expressive, animation-/plush-friendly).
- **Feet:** chunky, slightly oversized for stability and stomp/dance appeal; clear boot shape.

### COSTUME
- **Base outfit:** a cropped-at-natural-waist (age-appropriate, covers midriff with a white tee underneath) **gold idol jacket** with rolled sleeves + a clean white top + comfy purple shorts over **patterned leggings/tights** (modest), finished with short boots. Sporty-idol, not edgy.
- **Materials:** soft matte fabrics with a subtle satin sheen on the jacket; tiny embroidered star motifs; no metal studs/straps/edge.
- **Color blocking:** **gold jacket (primary) → white core → purple lower** — a clean top-to-bottom read; gold always frames the face.
- **Accessories:** the **Songstaff** (a friendly star-topped microphone-staff — *a tool to sing/read, never a weapon*), a small Lyric-Star earring, a gold braid-star tie.
- **Footwear:** rounded short boots, purple with a gold sole stripe; non-laced (toy-simple).
- **Signature motifs:** the **Lyric Star** + soundwave ribbons; tiny star buttons; lighthouse-beam gold glow.

**Design goal (3-size readability):**
- **App icon:** purple-braid silhouette + gold jacket + a single Lyric Star = readable at 48px.
- **Gameplay size:** face + gold jacket + braid pop against any district background.
- **Poster size:** holds up with embroidery, fabric sheen, eye-shine, and VFX detail.

## PHASE 3 — Turnaround Package

*Neutral A-pose, symmetrical except the braid (over left shoulder) and Songstaff (right hand).*

- **Front View:** symmetrical face/body; braid breaks symmetry forward-left; gold jacket centerline; Lyric-Star earring left ear. Establishes proportions & color blocking.
- **3/4 Front:** the **beauty/marketing angle** — shows braid depth, cheek volume, jacket collar, and the confident smile; use for hero portraits & store art.
- **Side:** profile of button nose + braid drape to mid-thigh; jacket-to-short silhouette; boot shape; check braid doesn't clip the body.
- **Back:** the braid is the star — center-back plait with star ties; jacket back panel carries an embroidered large Lyric Star (great for merch/back-of-figure).
- **3/4 Back:** shows braid volume + jacket hem + leg/boot transition; confirms hair-to-back deformation.

**Notes for Modelers:** ~4.7-head proportions; single watertight body mesh; braid as a separate jointed object (not cloth-sim heavy); hands 4-finger; keep silhouette shapes large/simple; avoid tiny floating accessories.
**Notes for Texture artists:** 2-band toon ramp; gold jacket on its own material for transformation emissive swaps; embroidered stars as texture (not geo); face uses a dedicated atlas region for crisp eyes; ASTC-friendly, atlas everything.
**Notes for Riggers:** Humanoid base for anim retarget; **braid chain (5–6 joints)** with secondary jiggle; expressive face rig (see Phase 4 blendshapes); jacket hem 2-bone flex; oversized hands need clean finger curls for "reading/pointing/mic-holding" poses.

**Symmetry:** body/face symmetric; **asymmetry = braid + earring + Songstaff hand** (intentional, keeps her dynamic).
**Unique silhouettes:** the **forward braid + star tie** and the **star-topped Songstaff** — both must survive at icon size.
**Deformation concerns:** braid–shoulder clipping during head turns; jacket-crop seam at the waist during bends; oversized hand finger-fan readability; cheek squash on big smiles (blendshape, not bone).

## PHASE 5 — Evolution System (5 stages)

*Learning Reading fills Rumi's Stardom meter; each threshold unlocks a transformation. Dramatically more exciting, always recognizably Rumi (purple braid + gold + Lyric Star persist).*

### Stage 1 — **Learning Trainee**
- **Visual:** the base Phase-2 look — everyday gold jacket, simple braid, plain Songstaff.
- **Costume:** rolled-sleeve jacket, white tee, purple shorts, short boots.
- **Color:** matte gold/purple/white.
- **VFX:** tiny sparkle when she reads a word right.
- **Emotional fantasy:** *"I'm starting my journey — I belong here."*
- **Unlock celebration:** Director Luna pins a Lyric-Star badge; soft chime + confetti.

### Stage 2 — **Rising Star**
- **Visual:** braid gains a woven **gold ribbon** + glowing star tie; jacket gets embroidered star trim; Songstaff tip lights softly.
- **Costume:** add a short shoulder cape-let; brighter gold.
- **Color:** gold begins to **emit** (subtle emissive); purple deepens.
- **VFX:** floating letters orbit her on a correct answer; light footstep sparkles.
- **Emotional fantasy:** *"People are starting to hear me!"*
- **Unlock celebration:** first transformation cutscene (light spiral → ribbon braids in → pose); crowd "ooh."

### Stage 3 — **Harmony Hero**
- **Visual:** full **stage jacket** with glowing star seams; the Songstaff **blooms light**; braid streams with gentle light.
- **Costume:** layered idol look, gold-lit cuffs/boots; small star epaulets.
- **Color:** gold glow now clearly luminous; white accents shimmer.
- **VFX:** a small **lighthouse beam** sweeps behind her; words she sings appear briefly in the air.
- **Emotional fantasy:** *"I can light up the whole harbor!"* (re-lights Harmony Harbor's lighthouse in story).
- **Unlock celebration:** big cutscene + the lighthouse relights + her song *"Read With Me"* plays.

### Stage 4 — **Spotlight Guardian**
- **Visual:** flowing **light-cloak made of drifting letters/lyrics**; crown-like star headpiece woven into the braid; Songstaff becomes a radiant star-staff.
- **Costume:** elegant performance gown-coat hybrid (still active/kid-appropriate), gold filigree of stars.
- **Color:** radiant gold + aurora highlights; purple as deep velvet.
- **VFX:** lyrics swirl and write themselves in the air around her; soft bloom + star motes.
- **Emotional fantasy:** *"I protect everyone's voice — no one is unheard."*
- **Unlock celebration:** spotlight descends, crowd chant, shareable (parent-gated) "Spotlight" pose.

### Stage 5 — **Legend Idol**
- **Visual:** peak radiance — the braid glows like a comet, a halo of **sky-written lyrics**, the Lyric Star blazing above the Songstaff; a living lighthouse-beam aura.
- **Costume:** legendary couture version of all prior cues (instantly "max level"), gold/white/purple at full luminance.
- **Color:** full emissive gold, aurora rim, sparkle particles.
- **VFX:** her lyrics write across the **whole sky** for the city to read along; grand bloom + confetti-stars.
- **Emotional fantasy:** *"Everyone has a song — and I helped them find it."*
- **Unlock celebration:** finale concert; full cast joins; song *"Everyone Has a Song"*; the city lights up. Saved to the Stardom Gallery to re-watch.

> **Continuity rule:** across all 5, keep the **forward purple braid, gold jacket lineage, and Lyric Star**. Each stage adds light/letters/star-couture — never changes her core identity.

**Recommendations:**

- **Lighting style:** baked GI + 1 dynamic key for the hero; warm key / cool fill / soft rim; bloom for sparkle. Calm-mode reduces bloom/particles.
- **Rendering approach:** URP Forward+, SRP Batcher, atlased materials, GPU-instanced critters, pooled VFX; characters drawn with shadow on hero only. Stay ≤~120 draw calls in-district.

## PHASE 10 — User Test Criteria (ages 5–7)

*Method: 1:1 play sessions + simple picture-based questions; small groups for "pick your favorite." Keep it playful; let them point, not read.*

### Appeal metrics
- **First-glance smile** (do they smile/lean in within 3s of seeing Rumi?) — observer-rated.
- **"Would you be friends with her?"** (yes/maybe/no, with picture buttons).
- **Pick-your-favorite** of the trio (Rumi/Mira/Zoey) — frequency & reasons.
- **Plush desire:** "Which would you want as a stuffie?" (Rumi vs Twinkle vs others).

### Comprehension metrics
- **"What is she good at?"** (Do they say reading/singing? Does the design *signal* her specialty?)
- **"What does she do?"** (hero/idol/helper — is the fantasy legible without text?)
- **Tool read:** do they see the Songstaff as a mic/friendly tool (good) or a weapon (fail)?

### Memorability metrics
- **24-hour recall:** next-day, can they name/point to Rumi and describe her (purple braid? gold? star?)?
- **Draw-it test:** ask them to draw her — which features survive (braid, star, gold) = the true brand cues.
- **Catchphrase stickiness:** do they repeat "Let's read our way there!" or a song line?

### Favorite-feature questions
- "What's your favorite thing about Rumi?" (hair / jacket / star / mic / Twinkle / her smile).
- "Which transformation is the coolest?" (show the 5-stage sheet, let them point).

### Transformation excitement scores
- **Reaction rating (1–5 smiley scale)** to each evolution stage reveal.
- **Stage-up anticipation:** after seeing Stage 5, do they ask "how do I get that?" (the learning hook working).
- **Re-watch desire:** do they ask to see the transformation again?

### Targets (go/no-go)
- ≥ **80%** first-glance positive; ≥ **70%** correctly infer "reading/singing helper"; ≥ **70%** next-day recall of ≥2 brand cues; Stage-5 excitement mean ≥ **4.3/5**; **0%** read the Songstaff as a weapon.

## Franchise-worthiness assessment

**Verdict: franchise-worthy.** Rumi hits every pillar — **warmth, aspirational confidence, emotional expressiveness, collectibility, and toyetic appeal** — with a crystal-clear, age-true power fantasy (reading/singing) and an unmistakable two-color silhouette + star symbol that survives from app icon to plush. The 5-stage evolution gives durable "I want to get there" pull tied directly to learning, and Twinkle is a plush slam-dunk.

**Recommendations to strengthen before lock:**
1. **Protect the Songstaff read** — prototype it at icon size with kids; if any child reads "weapon," round it further / add obvious mic-foam + star (the no-combat promise must be unmistakable).
2. **Make the braid the hero cue everywhere** (icon, plush, loading) — it's her strongest recall feature; never crop it out.
3. **Keep Stage 3 the "wow" affordability point** — ensure a typical learner reaches Harmony Hero in ~1–2 weeks so the big payoff isn't gated too far.
4. **Diversity & relatability:** offer the *player avatar* (not Rumi) wide skin-tone/hair customization so every child self-inserts while looking up to Rumi.
5. **Calm-mode variants** of her transformation VFX validated with sensory-sensitive kids.
6. **A11y/comprehension:** since she's the FTUE face, test that non-readers grasp her role purely from voice + visuals.

> Rumi should be the **first hero built** (vertical slice) and the **face of marketing** — she is the franchise's front door.

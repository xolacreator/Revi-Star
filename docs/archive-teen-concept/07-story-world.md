# 07 — Story & Worldbuilding

## 1. Premise

In the vertical megacity **Lumina Prime**, all light, sound, and shared feeling flow through the **Frequency** — a living network of ley-lines that the city literally sings on. When emotion is strong, the Frequency glows; when it dies, districts go dark. A creeping entropy called **the Hush** is spreading: it eats color, mutes sound, and erodes memory, leaving **gray husk-zones** and spawning **Discords** — echoes of forgotten songs twisted into monsters.

The only counter-force is **Resonance**: certain performers can convert raw emotion into light-and-sound magic. They form **labels** — hybrid idol groups and rescue squads — and headline **comebacks** that literally re-ignite the Frequency and push the Hush back.

The player is the new producer/leader of the underdog label **NOVA Division**, rebuilding a fallen scene one district at a time.

## 2. Tone & themes
- **Hopeful, high-energy, emotional.** Not grimdark. Stakes are real (memory loss, silenced cities) but the answer is connection, expression, and showing up for each other.
- Themes: **memory & identity, the courage to be seen, found-family, light vs. silence (not light vs. dark — Umbra heroes are good; the enemy is *absence*, the Hush).**
- Romance/rivalry subtext (e.g., Nova/Vesper) handled with restraint; relationships drive Duet mechanics.

Each district = a **biome** with its own palette, music genre, enemy sub-faction, and a hub-able "stage."

## 4. Narrative delivery (mobile-appropriate)
- **MV cutscenes** (pre-rendered-feel, runtime-rendered) at chapter beats — the "comeback" reveal, fully shows Concepts.
- **Live2D/3D VN dialogue** between stages (portraits + voice, skippable, auto-advance).
- **Encore co-op cutscenes** (Duet Links) double as character story payoff.
- **Backstage vignettes**: unlockable character side-stories (collection depth, voice-acted key lines).
- Keep mandatory reading short; deep lore is opt-in (Memory Vault codex).

## 6. Enemy lore → see 08
The Hush is not evil-with-a-plan so much as **entropy that learned to sing back**. Its avatar **Echo** believes silence is mercy (no pain if no memory). This gives the conflict emotional weight beyond "monsters."

## 7. Writing & content pipeline
- Narrative bible + character voice guides per Resonant (for VO consistency & loc).
- All dialogue in localizable tables; VO recorded in EN + KO + JA at launch, others text.
- Per-Season writers' room aligns the comeback theme song + chapter + Concept line + event arc.
- **Sensitivity & age rating:** target **Teen / 12+**; given a youthful K-pop audience, keep content within that rating, include the spending/parental safeguards in 04 & 09.

## 8. Theme music as canon
- Each Comeback ships a **title track**; songs are diegetic (idols literally perform them; Showcase = play the song).
- Music is a first-class asset class (see 11). Original compositions only; full rights cleared.

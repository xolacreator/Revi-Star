# Founder Testing — the lightweight version (first 3–5 kids)

> Not statistical confidence. Just: **"Can I get this in front of a few kids and learn something useful?"**
> Friends'/family/coworkers' kids. No recruiting, no surveys, no observer dashboards. **Just watch.**

## Before
1. Play it yourself a few times: `https://xolacreator.github.io/Revi-Star/` (sound on).
2. Hand the child the device at the "Tap to Start" screen. Sit beside them. Say once: *"There are no wrong answers — just have fun."* Then **be quiet**.

## Watch only three things (jot a word or two)
1. **Confusion** — in the first minute, do they start interacting on their own? Do they get Rumi / Twinkle / making their own hero? Where do they look to you for help?
2. **Excitement** — what actually lights them up? (Could be Twinkle, the stars, **making their avatar**, hearing their name, the lighthouse — not necessarily the transformation.)
3. **Replay interest** — the signal that matters most.

## After they stop, ask two things — then stop talking
- *"What was your favorite part?"* → whatever comes out **first** is the signal.
- *"Do you want to play it again tomorrow?"* → this tells you far more than "did you like it?" (kids say yes to everything).

## Then
- Identify the **single thing they cared about most** → double down on it next.
- That's it. Larger tests, D7, dashboards, monetization come later.

## Multi-day tip
The build gates to one day per calendar day (real "do they come back?"). To preview Day 2/3 yourself in one sitting: tap **👪 → 3-7-1 → Simulate next day**, or add `?day=2` / `?day=3` to the URL. Use `?reset=1` to start fresh for the next child.

# RELEASE NOTES — Harmony Harbor (Reventure)
_Player-facing summary per deployed version. Live channel: GitHub Pages._

## (no new build) — Studio Review Day (2026-07-14)
No player-facing changes today. The whole team-on-paper reviewed the game:
nine specialist reports now live in `docs/`, and the next five updates are
planned (doors that open into lessons, sounds for everything you touch, living
water and shooting stars, story missions). Current live build remains **v88**.

## v88 — The Star Shop opens ⭐ (2026-07-14)
- New: the lavender house is now the **Star Shop** — tap it and your Star Hunter
  walks to the door and steps inside.
- New: earn ⭐ for every correct answer and every sparkle you collect.
- Buy: a Firework Show over the harbor, Gold/Pink/Sky sparkle trails for your
  hero, Party Balloons for the plaza, and a bow Twinkle wears forever.

## v87 — The whole band is in town 🎤
- Mira and Zoey now hang out near their houses — they dance, wave, watch you
  walk by, and say hello in their own voices when tapped.
- Everything in the plaza responds to touch: trees, lamps, benches, planters,
  crates and crystals pop and sparkle.

## v86 — One beautiful show
- Twinkle's Star Check now happens on the same concert stage as lessons.
- Transformation moments show the character glowing up, storybook-style.

## v85 — Harmony Harbor comes alive
- A real lighthouse: striped tower, glowing windows, gallery, starred roof.
- The town square is dressed: street lamps, festival flags, benches, flower
  planters, and six swaying trees (one blossoms pink).
- Houses have warm lit windows; the camera pulls back to show the neon skyline.

## v84 — Twinkle behaves
- Twinkle is smaller and flies beside you instead of blocking the view.

## v83 — The Concert Stage update
- Brand-new title screen: Rumi under the spotlights, "Take the Stage 🎤".
- Lessons happen on a dark neon stage with glowing answer pads.
- Level-ups are warm storybook moments; rewards fall like an aurora.

## v79–v82 — Polish + the fix that made updates appear
- New fonts, glass panels, gradient buttons, sparkle celebrations, animated
  question entrances — and a caching fix so phones actually receive updates.

## v74–v78 — The learning engine grows up
- 21 questions a day across 9 reading skills, easier early and harder later.
- Every question is spoken by real character voices (175 clips).
- Fixed: game no longer sticks after a correct answer; no more copy/paste
  pop-up while steering.

## v89–v92 — The game gets doors, sounds, and sparkle (2026-07-16)
- **Doors!** A star-iris swirls you into lessons and the shop — no more
  sudden jumps.
- **Your stars fly.** Every right answer sends stars zooming into a filling
  star bar (no more numbers to read), with a confetti party every 7 stars.
- **Everything makes a sound.** Benches knock, lamps ting, trees rustle,
  crystals chime; the shop rings its doorbell, fireworks boom, and the
  harbor gently laps and gulls call while you explore.
- Faster + cleaner: the app is almost half the download it was; the top bar
  no longer squishes its buttons together.

# Child Development Specialist

## Mandate
Advocate for the actual five-year-old holding the phone. Veto anything that
requires reading to navigate, fine motor precision, working-memory juggling, or
that punishes. Attention span budget: 10–15 minutes/session.

## Owns
- Age-appropriateness review of every interaction and screen
- Reading-barrier audit (any UI text a pre-reader must decode)
- Motor audit (touch target sizes, drag precision, timing windows)
- Emotional safety (no fail states, encouragement tone, parent gate)

## Quality bar
PBS Kids / Khan Kids compliance instincts: narrate everything, one clear action
per moment, forgiveness everywhere, delight without overstimulation.

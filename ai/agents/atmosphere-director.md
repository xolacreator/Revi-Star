# Atmosphere Director

## Mandate
Own the feeling of *being there*: sky, weather, lighting states, ambient life,
particles, time-of-day mood. The harbor should feel alive when the child does
nothing at all.

## Owns
- Sky dome, clouds, fog, gloom→bloom arc, lighthouse halo
- Ambient life: butterflies, birds, motes, footstep flowers, ripples, tree sway
- Lighting states (pre-bloom, bloomed, transform spotlight)
- Idle-world choreography (lamp breathing, crystal pulse, glyph rotation)

## Quality bar
Ghibli-adjacent stillness: standing still should be pleasant for 30 seconds.

## Current assessment — 6.5/10
Good ambient census (7 butterflies, 4 birds, 90 motes, swaying trees, breathing
lamps). Weaknesses: single static lighting state after bloom; no weather; sky
is a painted still; bloom is one-shot and its magic never recurs.

## Top asks
1. "Harmony hour" — subtle 3-state color cycle (dawn/day/dusk tint) keyed to
   real clock so returning at different times looks different.
2. Occasional shooting star + wish sparkle (tap = bonus star) — retention magic.
3. Gentle firefly ring around the lighthouse after the day's lesson completes.

# Gameplay Director

## Mandate
Own moment-to-moment play: movement, camera, interaction, objectives, reward
loops. Objectives always obvious to a five-year-old. Reward loops create
anticipation ("what do I get tomorrow?").

## Owns
- Tap/hold-drag steering, path targets, marker, avatar reactions
- Camera framing, easing, cinematic moments (`CAM_OFF`, `cineUntil`, spotlight)
- World interactions: prop taps, NPC greetings, sparkle collection, shop entry
- Star economy (earn rates vs. prices) and progression pacing

## Quality bar
Pokémon Let's Go clarity, Animal Crossing gentleness. One finger plays
everything. Nothing requires reading to navigate.

## Current assessment — 6.5/10
Explore + tap-delight + shop loop is real gameplay. Weaknesses: camera is a
fixed offset (no ease-in, anticipation, or gentle shake); no in-world quest
beyond "walk to the glow"; stars currently have exactly one sink (shop) and no
daily anticipation hook.

## Top asks
1. Camera feel pass: eased follow, slight look-ahead while dragging, soft push-in
   at doors/lighthouse, micro-shake on fireworks.
2. Daily "surprise crate" at the dock (1 free shop item tease → anticipation).
3. Quest breadcrumbs: Twinkle flies toward today's objective when idle.

# Art Director

## Mandate
Own the visual identity: the Claude Design system (Concert Stage / Storybook /
Aurora Glass), character presentation, color, typography, and the bar that no
placeholder art ships. Eliminate the prototype look, screen by screen.

## Owns
- Design tokens in `harbor.css` (:root), Fredoka/Nunito/Baloo 2 hierarchy
- Screen skins: intro, challenge, star check, shop, transform, reward, parent

- Art asset pipeline with founder (coach PNGs, title art, GLB characters)

designed by the same studio on the same day.
